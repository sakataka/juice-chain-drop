## Style Prompt

Bright arcade puzzle promo for Juice Chain Drop. Use the existing game's warm paper, fruit, and juice-shop feeling, with crisp readable Japanese captions and rounded-but-controlled UI shapes. The game screen is always the hero; decoration supports it without covering the board.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#fff7dc`
- Fruit red: `#e94b4b`
- Citrus orange: `#f28a28`
- Juice green: `#1c8f78`
- Ink navy: `#16324a`
- Cream panel: `#fffaf0`

## Typography

- Headline: `Fredoka`, rounded and bold
- Body and captions: system sans-serif, heavy weight for readability

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make the game screen small.
- Do not use long explanatory paragraphs.
- Do not cover the board with captions.
- Do not use dark, techy, or monochrome styling.
- Do not exaggerate mechanics that are not present in the game.
